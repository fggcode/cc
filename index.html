Bakery questions, answered

Not seeing yours? Call [phone] or send a note.

How far ahead should I order a custom cake?

Order a custom cake at Bloom and Butter at least 72 hours ahead. For weekends and holidays, a week is safer. Smaller cakes can sometimes be done sooner, so call and ask.

Do you have nut free or gluten free treats?

We have nut free cookies every day, made with care in a kitchen that also uses nuts. Gluten free options rotate weekly. Ask at the counter for today's list.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. Order online by 6pm and pick up the next morning from 7am. Your order waits at the counter with your name on it.

Do you cater office events?

Yes, we make cookie and pastry boxes for 10 to 100 people. Give us two days notice and we can deliver in East Nashville.
